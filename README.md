# STICKMAN: SHADOW WORLD

An original 3D mobile open-world adventure project built with Godot 4.4.1.

## Current prototype systems
- Cinematic illustrated startup/loading screen with staged progress, then a real main menu with Play, Continue, graphics settings and Exit before gameplay begins.
- A procedural asphalt street grid through the districts, raised sidewalks, curb strips and painted crosswalks around the main junction.
- Town trees with independently phased wind sway, animated leaf-cluster vertex motion, textured bark and layered foliage.
- Procedural high-detail noise textures for terrain, asphalt, building plaster and plaza stone, with triplanar mapping for consistent surfaces.
- Procedurally modeled birds with individual flight orbits, body banking and continuously flapping wings over the central district; the current birds are lightweight game meshes, not scanned or photorealistic animal assets.
- Two detailed stone staircases with step collision, edge trims, handrails, raised landings and adjacent walkable ramps.
- Two collision-backed rolling terrain mounds with smoothly sampled height meshes and scattered rock formations for real elevation changes beyond the staircases.
- A purchasable travel cape at the Supply Shop; its subdivided cloth mesh ripples with wind, sprint speed and airborne movement, and can be equipped or stored. This is a lightweight shader-driven cloth effect, not a full cloth-physics simulation.
- Shoes are parented to the leg pivots so the feet follow the procedural walk/run cycle.
- Eight moving traffic cars with separate paint colors, glass cabins, rolling wheels, lights and collision bodies; cars follow lanes and stop at the signal cycle.
- Four traffic-light posts with glowing red, amber and green lenses, synchronized signal phases and pedestrian crossing markings.
- Ten stylized humanoid residents with clothing colors, hair, hands, shoes and simple opposing arm/leg walk cycles; their wandering is kept near sidewalk areas.
- Town street lamps and a furnished starter townhouse interior.
- Working entrance and exit portals between the city and the townhouse.
- Dynamic directional sunlight and a running world clock.
- Procedural moving 3D cloud formations with layered silhouettes and slow atmospheric drift.
- Filmic tone mapping, subtle glow and distance fog, with mobile-friendly quality scaling.
- Four graphics presets: performance, balanced, high visual and cinematic maximum.
- Custom 3D Stickman assembled from meshes, with procedural walk, sprint, airborne and landing poses.
- Landscape-first mobile orientation and responsive wide-screen HUD/control layout.
- First-person / third-person camera switching with free vertical and horizontal look.
- Configurable look sensitivity, field of view, third-person camera distance and inverted vertical look.
- Pause menu, settings panel, graphics quality presets, FPS caps and persistent local settings.
- Resizable touch controls with left-hand/right-hand layout selection.
- Walking, sprinting, stamina, jumping, contextual wall jumps and obstacle vaults.
- Touchscreen controls, right-side touch camera look, and desktop keyboard controls.
- HUD with coins, XP, stamina, interaction prompts, world time, wallet cash, bank-card balance and wanted level.
- Minecraft-style 9-slot quick hotbar always visible, plus an 18-slot backpack/chest grid with item swapping.
- Separate wallet cash and bank-card balance, persistent save data, and an interactable city card reader that deposits wallet cash.
- A small supermarket set with shelves, a one-shift paid job, and a fictional unpaid-item event that triggers the city response system.
- A non-lethal police pursuit and arrest loop; residents flee when the player is nearby during an alert.
- A home storage chest that opens the inventory and lets items be swapped between the hotbar and storage.
- Procedural ambient wind, footsteps, card-reader confirmation and alert tones generated by the in-game audio manager.
- Interactable mission board, job office, supply shop and purchasable townhouse.
- Ten wandering residents with dialogue and basic movement AI.
- Local save/load system using the device's user data directory.

## Controls
- Move: WASD / arrow keys
- Sprint: Shift
- Jump: Space; press jump again in the air near an obstacle for a parkour move
- Switch camera: V
- Pause / settings: Escape or the on-screen MENU button
- Interact: E
- Inventory: I or the on-screen BAG button
- Save: F5
- Load: F9
- Look: mouse or drag on the right side of the screen
- Android: on-screen controls

## Android target
The project uses Godot's mobile GL Compatibility renderer. Android export configuration, device testing and APK packaging are still pending. No APK is produced by the current validation workflow.

2. Expand interiors into multiple enterable buildings with rooms, stairs and interactive furniture.
3. Improve NPC routines, dialogue, schedules, reputation and city reactions.
4. Complete jobs, shops, trading, crafting, inventory UI and balanced economy.
5. Build a full story campaign, side quests, puzzles and progression.
6. Add vehicles and transportation.
7. Expand the procedural material library with authored PBR albedo/normal/roughness maps, improve foliage silhouettes and add weather, original art, sound and music.
8. Profile performance on real Android devices, configure Android export, build APKs, test installation and prepare release signing.

This repository contains an expanding prototype with connected starter gameplay systems. It is not yet the complete open-world game in the design brief. High-end production quality requires original art and animation, substantial content creation, iterative testing and mobile optimization.
